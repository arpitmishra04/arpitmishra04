01

Now

Where the hours go

I'm an engineer at Ryan, LLC, working on the Owner Claims Portal inside Tracker Pro — the property tax software used by the people who file, track and resolve owner claims.

Tax workflows are unforgiving. The data has to survive an audit two years after anyone remembers entering it, and the screen in front of it has to stay readable to people who don't think in schemas. Most of my day is spent making those two requirements agree with each other.

Before this, two years at Tezo — first shipping HRMS features for Keka across three PODs, then GenAI work for Habasit. That second stretch taught me the thing I still lead with: a language model isn't a feature until you can put a number on it.

A model is a dependency, not a feature. Validation in front of it, evals behind it, and a metric somebody else can check.
